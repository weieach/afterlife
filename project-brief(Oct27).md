An interactive archive for modern paper effigies in Chinese funerary practice, masquerading as a “shopping site”

Based on a thousand-year folk tradition of Chinese ancestral worship: During Qingming (Tomb-sweeping day), people burn paper models of money and goods to their deceased relative. The idea is that burning them sends these goods to the afterlife so deceased people are comfortable and provided for
Most common offering being spirit money / hell bank notes, which are paper money used in afterlife. There’s also ingots and money tree
Aside from different forms of money, people burn paper models of e.g. food, clothing and shoes, covering basic living necessities
However, a modernized version of this tradition evolved to include a new diversity of goods, including smartphones, luxury bags, helicopters, etc

Content of site: 
- Landing page as an editorial to “market the good”
- Product pages
    Instead of “add to card”, each product page offers a “download” button allowing visitors to download the necessary pdfs to create the paper effigies at home (low-poly style) - hypothetically they can “follow the tradition” by burning them

- an interactive data collection portion (will be its own site at the site when the main site is not made; refer to as "survey" for short) to ask people what kind of objects they personally choose to bring from a large catalogue

Themes: 
Commentary on material and consumer culture, exclusivity of luxury and status symbol → what constitutes our ultimate material aspiration
the goal isn’t to critique the tradition or the superstition around it, but rather to acknowledge how it materializes a visual representation of the changing conscious desires of a society
an age of disenchantment (Max Weber)
a society being more capable than ever to satisfy basic needs
Banality of commodification: in the past there were luxury items too, but none were so timely and relevant that it drew laugh from people
What do we want for ourselves when given infinite resources?

Challenge:

offer an updated re-intepretation
Aesthetics & visual treatment: more traditional / more modern?

Target audience: 
All user research will be conducted on Chinese community (native citizens & immigrant), but the resultant project is to be made understandable to a global audience
establish empathy & shared understanding; a way to communicate to people that more similarities is to be found in material desire than differences
